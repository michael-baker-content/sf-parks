## Start broad, then narrow the list

The latest Explore update adds nine broad areas, including Richmond & Presidio, Sunset & Westside, and Mission, Bernal & Noe. These are practical browsing groups rather than official boundaries, and they offer a quicker starting point when a neighborhood-by-neighborhood list feels too detailed.

## Compare destinations with useful thresholds

You can now set a minimum number of amenities or a minimum park size in acres. Park acreage comes from the official property data published by the City of San Francisco, while amenity totals reflect the features currently represented in this project.

- Use a minimum amenity count to find destinations with more options in one place.
- Use minimum acreage when you want room for a longer visit or a broader landscape.
- Choose Most amenities to bring feature-rich destinations to the top of the results.

## Share what you find

Your search, filters, thresholds, area selections, and sort choice remain in the page address. Copying that address lets someone else open the same Explore view without rebuilding it from scratch.

The underlying information is still incomplete in places, so each destination page links to official sources for details that need confirmation. This update is meant to make the available evidence easier to navigate, not to imply that every park has been documented equally.
